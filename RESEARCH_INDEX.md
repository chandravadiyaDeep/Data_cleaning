Category-specific end-to-end workflows, plus rule-based, statistical, domain-driven, ML-based, AI-assisted, human-in-the-loop, probabilistic, incremental, continuous, hybrid, and adaptive decision strategies.

## Research principles

- Research before implementation.
- Distinguish detection, diagnosis, recommendation, transformation, and validation.
- Never assume a cleaning operation is universally correct.
- Preserve original data and document every transformation.
- Record uncertainty, limitations, risks, and human-review requirements.
- Prefer primary sources and reproducible experiments.
- Clearly distinguish strict data cleaning from downstream ML preprocessing.
